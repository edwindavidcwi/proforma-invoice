Parent & Teacher Pack

A learn-while-you-play game for Ethan — how to play, plus every question

🌟What is this game?

It looks like a Game Boy adventure, but it's really a learning game. As your child plays and battles, the game asks a school question — answer it right and the move works! Get it wrong and it gives a friendly hint and lets you try again. Nobody is ever punished for a mistake.

💚 The goal: Ethan practises reading, maths, and more without it feeling like homework.

▶️Getting started

  1. Open Pokemon Quiz (the Windows app, or the app on the phone).
  2. Press Start (the Enter key, or tap Start) to begin a new game and follow the short intro.
  3. Walk around with the arrow keys. When a battle begins, choose FIGHT then a move — that's when a question pops up!

❓How a question works

This is what a question looks like on screen:

Move the ▶ arrow to your answer, then press A.
  1. Read the question at the top (or let the game read it aloud — see below).
  2. Use the arrow keys to move the ▶ arrow to the answer you want.
  3. Press A (the Z key) to choose.
  4. Right answer ✅ — your move lands and you build a streak. Wrong ❌ — a hint appears and you try once more.

🗺️Your Pokémon adventure

You are a young trainer starting out in your home town. Your big quest: travel across the land, earn 8 gym badges, and become the Pokémon Champion! 🏆 Along the way you catch and train Pokémon — and every move you make is powered by answering a school question.

💚 You can't lose for good. If your Pokémon faint, you wake up safe at the last town with full health and no money lost. So it's always safe to explore!
💾 Save often! Press Start → SAVE. The game does not save by itself.

🥚Pick your starter Pokémon

Professor Oak lets you pick one of three little Pokémon to start. Each one is good — here's what's easy or tricky about each:

🌿 Bulbasaur

Easiest start 💚

A friendly plant-dinosaur. Strong against the first 2 gyms (Rock + Water). Great pick for new players!

Grows into: Ivysaur → Venusaur

🔥 Charmander

Hard start 🔥

A fire-lizard with a flame on its tail. Weak against the first 2 gyms. But late in the game it becomes super strong!

Grows into: Charmeleon → Charizard 🐉

🐢 Squirtle

Balanced 👍

A tiny turtle that shoots water. Strong against the first gym (Brock). A safe, all-round pick.

Grows into: Wartortle → Blastoise

Our pick for Ethan: 🌿 Bulbasaur — the first few hours feel really easy and he'll see lots of quick wins.

👟The complete journey — stop by stop

Follow these in order. Each stop tells you exactly what to do, the Pokémon you might catch, and the best tip for that part of the game.

Stop 1 · Your home🏠 Pallet Town

  • Walk up to Professor Oak's lab.
  • Pick your starter Pokémon (see above).
  • Your rival battles you right away — if you lose, that's OK, you heal up.
  • Pop next door — Daisy (rival's sister) gives you a free Town Map 🗺️ so you can see where you are.
Pokémon you might catch here: Nothing wild here — head north!

Stop 2 · Your first journey🌾 Route 1 → Viridian City

  • Walk through tall grass — that's where wild Pokémon hide. Press A on a Poké Ball to catch one!
  • In Viridian City, the Pokémart clerk gives you Oak's Parcel.
  • Walk back to Pallet and hand it to Oak. He gives you the Pokédex!
  • Return to Viridian and buy Poké Balls (to catch) and Potions (to heal).
Pokémon you might catch here: Pidgey 🐦, Rattata 🐀

Stop 3 · The bug forest🐛 Route 2 → Viridian Forest → Pewter City

  • Head north through the forest. Bug Catcher trainers will challenge you — great practice!
  • Look out for a wild ⚡ Pikachu — rare but possible.
  • Pick up free Poké Balls on the ground (look for ! marks).
  • Out of the forest → you reach Pewter City.
Pokémon you might catch here: Caterpie 🐛, Weedle 🐛, Pikachu ⚡ (rare!)

GYM 1 · Boulder Badge 🏅🪨 Pewter Gym — Brock

  • Brock uses Rock-type Pokémon: Geodude and Onix.
  • How to win easily: use 🌿 Grass moves (Bulbasaur!) or 💧 Water moves. They do 4× damage!
  • Don't use ⚡ Electric — it can't hurt Rock at all.
  • Win → you get Badge 1: Boulder Badge and the move TM34 Bide.
✨ The Pewter Museum of Science is fun to walk through (free if you go in the side door).

Stop 4 · The cave full of Zubats🌙 Route 3 → Mt. Moon → Route 4

  • On Route 3 you'll fight lots of trainers. Get strong before the cave!
  • Inside Mt. Moon: lots of wild Zubat and Geodude. Try to catch a Clefairy ⭐ — it's rare and cute.
  • Team Rocket grunts are here — the bad guys! Defeat them.
  • At the end you must choose one fossil: Helix (becomes Omanyte) or Dome (becomes Kabuto). You can only ever have one!
  • Pop out into Route 4 → Cerulean City.
Pokémon you might catch here: Zubat 🦇, Geodude 🪨, Clefairy ⭐ (rare!), Paras 🍄
💡 Pick whichever fossil sounds cooler — you can revive it later on Cinnabar Island.

GYM 2 · Cascade Badge 🏅💧 Cerulean Gym — Misty

  • Misty uses Water-type Pokémon: Staryu and Starmie.
  • How to win easily: 🌿 Grass moves or ⚡ Electric moves. Pikachu finally shines!
  • Avoid 🔥 Fire moves — water snuffs them out.
  • Win → Badge 2: Cascade Badge and TM11 BubbleBeam.

Stop 5 · The 5-trainer bridge🌉 Nugget Bridge → Bill's House

  • Just north of Cerulean is Nugget Bridge: beat 5 trainers in a row to win a gold Nugget (worth 5000!).
  • At the end, your Rival battles you again.
  • Keep going — you'll reach Bill's House. He's stuck as a Pokémon!
  • Help Bill (just press the buttons he asks) → he gives you the SS Anne Ticket for a big cruise ship!
Pokémon you might catch here: Bellsprout 🌿, Oddish 🌿, Abra 🔮 (super rare!)

Stop 6 · The big boat🚢 SS Anne — the cruise ship

  • Show your ticket at Vermilion City dock. The ship has lots of trainers — great for levelling up!
  • Find the Captain's room at the back. Talk to him → he gives you the move HM01 Cut ✂️.
  • Teach Cut to a Pokémon → now you can chop little trees and open new paths!
💡 Don't leave the ship until you have Cut — the ship sails away soon!

GYM 3 · Thunder Badge 🏅⚡ Vermilion Gym — Lt. Surge

  • Lt. Surge uses Electric-type Pokémon: Voltorb, Pikachu, Raichu.
  • How to win easily: use 🌋 Ground moves — electric can't hit ground Pokémon at all!
  • Catch a Diglett or Sandshrew for this fight (Diglett's Cave is right outside!).
  • Trash-can puzzle: first find the right trash can, then find the second one right next to it. Wrong = reset!
  • Win → Badge 3: Thunder Badge and TM24 Thunderbolt.
✨ In Vermilion, the Pokémon Fan Club President gives you a free Bike Voucher — trade it in Cerulean for a free Bicycle 🚲!

Stop 7 · The dark cave🔦 Route 9 → Rock Tunnel

  • Rock Tunnel is pitch black! You need the move HM05 Flash 🔦 to see.
  • Get Flash from Oak's Aide on Route 2 (he checks how many Pokémon you've caught — needs about 10).
  • Teach Flash, then walk through the tunnel. Lots of trainers and wild Pokémon!
  • Come out the south side → Lavender Town.
Pokémon you might catch here: Geodude 🪨, Onix 🪨, Machop 🥊, Cubone 💀
⚠️ Skipping Flash makes the cave super hard — one step at a time blind!

Stop 8 · The ghost tower👻 Lavender Town → Pokémon Tower

  • Lavender Town has a tall scary tower with Ghost Pokémon. But you can't see them yet!
  • You'll come back here later with a Silph Scope to see the ghosts.
  • For now, just visit and head west toward Celadon.
⚠️ Going up the tower without the Silph Scope — you'll get blocked!

Stop 9 · The big shopping city🌀 Route 7 → Celadon City

  • Celadon is the biggest city. It has a huge Department Store 🏬 — lots of items, healing, and TMs!
  • There are vending machines at the top of the store — buy Fresh Water, Soda Pop, Lemonade. Save one!
  • The Game Corner 🎰 has slot machines — and a secret Team Rocket hideout behind a poster!
  • Talk to the man in the Celadon Mansion's apartment → he gives you a free Eevee 🦊!
Pokémon you might catch here: Eevee 🦊 (gift!), Vulpix 🦊, Growlithe 🐕

GYM 4 · Rainbow Badge 🏅🌿 Celadon Gym — Erika

  • Erika uses Grass-type Pokémon: Victreebel, Tangela, Vileplume.
  • How to win easily: 🔥 Fire, 🦅 Flying, or 🐛 Bug moves — all are very strong vs Grass!
  • Win → Badge 4: Rainbow Badge and TM21 Mega Drain.

Stop 10 · Bust the bad guys🥷 Rocket Hideout (under Game Corner)

  • In the Game Corner, look for a poster on the wall — press A on it → secret stairs!
  • Fight your way down 4 floors of Rocket grunts.
  • Boss fight: Giovanni (round 1) — uses Ground Pokémon. Use Water or Grass.
  • Win → you get the Silph Scope 👻 (lets you see ghosts) and the Lift Key.

Stop 11 · Save Mr. Fuji👻 Pokémon Tower (Lavender)

  • Climb the tower with the Silph Scope. You can now see ghost Pokémon — lots of Gastly and Haunter!
  • Try to catch one — ghosts are awesome.
  • Near the top: a sad Marowak ghost blocks the way. Beat her in a battle.
  • Defeat the Rocket grunts at the top, save kindly Mr. Fuji.
  • He gives you the Poké Flute 🎵 — wakes up sleeping Snorlax!
Pokémon you might catch here: Gastly 👻, Haunter 👻, Cubone 💀

Stop 12 · The big sleepy lump😪 Wake the Snorlax

  • Two huge sleeping Snorlax 🐻 block the roads — one on Route 12 (south of Lavender), one on Route 16 (west of Celadon).
  • Walk up to one, press A, choose Poké Flute from your bag.
  • Snorlax wakes up and battles you! It's a tough fight — or try to catch it!
  • Now the road is open!
Pokémon you might catch here: Snorlax 🐻 (one-of-a-kind)
💡 Try to catch Snorlax! It's huge and very strong — one of the best Pokémon in the game.

Stop 13 · The Safari Zone city🌺 Routes 12-15 → Fuchsia City

  • Long walk south through tall grass and forests — lots of trainers and Pokémon.
  • Reach Fuchsia City — home of the famous Safari Zone 🦒!
  • Safari Zone: pay 500 coins, get 30 special Poké Balls + 600 steps. Catch rare Pokémon: Tauros, Chansey, Scyther, Pinsir, Dratini, Kangaskhan!
  • Important hidden items in Safari Zone: find HM03 Surf 🌊 (Area 3 secret house) and the Gold Teeth (Area 3 grass).
  • Bring the Gold Teeth to the Warden (next to the Pokémon Center) → he gives you HM04 Strength 💪!
Pokémon you might catch here: Tauros 🐄, Dratini 🐲, Scyther 🦓, Kangaskhan 🦙, Chansey 💕
💡 Surf lets you travel on water. Strength pushes giant boulders. Both unlock new areas!

GYM 5 · Soul Badge 🏅☠️ Fuchsia Gym — Koga

  • Koga uses Poison-type Pokémon: Koffing, Muk, Weezing.
  • How to win easily: 🔮 Psychic moves or 🌋 Ground moves — both are very strong!
  • The gym is full of invisible walls — bump into them to find the path.
  • Win → Badge 5: Soul Badge and TM06 Toxic.

Stop 14 · Tower full of bad guys🏙️ Saffron City → Silph Co.

  • Saffron's gates were blocked by a thirsty guard! Bring a Fresh Water / Soda / Lemonade from Celadon vending machines — give him a drink → he steps aside.
  • Team Rocket has taken over the Silph Co. building (11 floors of bad guys!).
  • Find the Card Key on floor 5 to open locked doors.
  • A man on the 7th floor gives you Lapras 💙 — a powerful Water/Ice Pokémon!
  • Top floor: beat your Rival, then beat Giovanni round 2.
  • Win → the Silph president gives you the Master Ball — catches any Pokémon, never misses! Save it for Mewtwo!
Pokémon you might catch here: Lapras 💙 (gift!)
💡 Don't use the Master Ball on a normal Pokémon — save it for the strongest!

GYM 6 · Marsh Badge 🏅🔮 Saffron Gym — Sabrina

  • Sabrina uses Psychic-type Pokémon: Kadabra, Alakazam, Mr. Mime, Venomoth.
  • How to win easily: 🐛 Bug moves are super strong (Pinsir, Scyther, Beedrill!).
  • The gym has teleporter tiles on the floor — step on them to jump rooms. Try each one to find the path.
  • Win → Badge 6: Marsh Badge and TM46 Psywave.

Stop 15 · Surf to the south🌐 Routes 19-20 → Seafoam Islands → Cinnabar

  • From Fuchsia, head south and Surf on the water!
  • Seafoam Islands: a cave with water inside. Use Strength to push boulders into the water current to stop it.
  • Deep inside: catch the legendary ice-bird Articuno ❄️!
  • Out the other side → swim south to Cinnabar Island 🌋.
Pokémon you might catch here: Articuno ❄️ (legendary!), Seel 🦭, Krabby 🦀, Tentacool 🐙

Stop 16 · The burning mansion🏚️ Cinnabar Island → Pokémon Mansion

  • On Cinnabar there's a Lab that revives fossils (give them your fossil from Mt. Moon!).
  • The Pokémon Mansion is creepy and locked. Find switches on each floor (read the journals → hints about Mewtwo!).
  • Find the Secret Key on the basement floor.
  • Take it to Blaine's gym — the door is now open!
Pokémon you might catch here: Growlithe 🐕, Ponyta 🐎, Magmar 🔥, Grimer 🦠

GYM 7 · Volcano Badge 🏅🔥 Cinnabar Gym — Blaine

  • Blaine uses Fire-type Pokémon: Growlithe, Ponyta, Rapidash, Arcanine.
  • How to win easily: 💧 Water moves are perfect (Squirtle, Lapras!). 🌋 Ground or 🪨 Rock also work great.
  • The gym has trivia doors (the mod's quiz takes care of these!).
  • Win → Badge 7: Volcano Badge and TM38 Fire Blast.

GYM 8 · Earth Badge 🏅🌋 Viridian Gym — Giovanni

  • Fly or bike back to Viridian City — the gym is finally open!
  • Giovanni is the Team Rocket boss! He uses Ground/Rock Pokémon: Rhyhorn, Dugtrio, Nidoking, Nidoqueen, Rhydon.
  • How to win easily: 💧 Water, 🌿 Grass, 🧊 Ice, or 🥊 Fighting moves.
  • Win → Badge 8: Earth Badge and TM27 Fissure. Giovanni quits Team Rocket!
  • Now you can go to Victory Road and the Pokémon League!
💡 Before Victory Road: level your team to about 50. Get plenty of Potions, Revives, Full Heals!

Stop 17 · The final mountain🌈 Route 23 → Victory Road

  • Show all 8 badges to the guards on Route 23 → they let you pass.
  • Victory Road is a giant cave puzzle: use Strength to push boulders onto floor switches to open doors.
  • Lots of strong wild Pokémon and trainers!
  • Legendary catch: deep inside, you'll find Moltres 🔥 the legendary fire-bird!
  • Out the top → Indigo Plateau!
Pokémon you might catch here: Moltres 🔥 (legendary!), Onix 🪨, Machoke 🥊, Marowak 💀
💡 Save right before each Elite Four fight! There are NO Pokémon Centers inside — only one big save spot at the entrance.

⚔️Type cheat-sheet (what beats what)

Pick a move whose type is strong against the enemy's type and you do 2× or 4× damage. The colour cards below are the easy ones to remember:

🔥 FireStrong against: Burns Grass 🌿, Bug 🐛, Ice 🧊Hurts itself against: Water, Rock, Ground
💧 WaterStrong against: Splashes Fire 🔥, Rock 🪨, Ground 🌋Hurts itself against: Grass, Electric
🌿 GrassStrong against: Grows over Water 💧, Rock 🪨, Ground 🌋Hurts itself against: Fire, Flying, Bug, Ice, Poison
⚡ ElectricStrong against: Zaps Water 💧, Flying 🦅Doesn't hit Ground at all!
🌋 GroundStrong against: Quakes Fire 🔥, Electric ⚡, Rock 🪨, Poison ☠️Can't hit Flying Pokémon
🦅 FlyingStrong against: Swoops Grass 🌿, Bug 🐛, Fighting 🥊Hurts itself against: Electric, Rock, Ice
🔮 PsychicStrong against: Mind-blasts Fighting 🥊, Poison ☠️Hurts itself against: Bug
🐛 BugStrong against: Bites Grass 🌿, Psychic 🔮Hurts itself against: Fire, Flying, Rock
🧊 IceStrong against: Freezes Grass 🌿, Ground 🌋, Flying 🦅, Dragon 🐉Hurts itself against: Fire, Water, Rock
🥊 FightingStrong against: Punches Normal, Rock 🪨, Ice 🧊Doesn't hit Ghost at all!
🪨 RockStrong against: Smashes Fire 🔥, Flying 🦅, Bug 🐛, Ice 🧊Hurts itself against: Water, Grass, Ground, Fighting
👻 GhostStrong against: Spooks Ghost 👻, Psychic 🔮Doesn't hit Normal at all!
💡 Easy starter rule: Water beats Fire 🔥, Fire beats Grass 🌿, Grass beats Water 💧. Always have one of each on your team!

🌟The 5 Special Moves (HMs) — how to find each

HMs are special moves that don't just work in battle — they work on the world too. Teach them to your Pokémon to unlock new paths!

HM01 Cut ✂️

Chops down little trees that block paths.

📍 Get it from: SS Anne — the Captain gives it to you after he's seasick (Vermilion City dock).

HM02 Fly 🦅

Lets you instantly fly to any town you've been to!

📍 Get it from: Route 16 — in a small house just west of Celadon City (wake Snorlax first).

HM03 Surf 🌊

Lets you ride your Water Pokémon across the sea.

📍 Get it from: Safari Zone — secret house in Area 3 (the far corner of the Safari Zone).

HM04 Strength 💪

Pushes giant boulders around (needed in Seafoam, Victory Road).

📍 Get it from: Fuchsia City Warden — bring him the Gold Teeth from the Safari Zone.

HM05 Flash 🔦

Lights up dark caves so you can see!

📍 Get it from: Oak's Aide on Route 2 — he wants you to have caught about 10 different Pokémon first.

✨Legendary Pokémon to find

There are 4 super-rare Pokémon in the game — you only get one chance to catch each! Save right before you find them. (Throw your Master Ball at the strongest one!)

  • ❄️ Articuno — ice-bird, deep inside Seafoam Islands. Use Surf + Strength.
  • ⚡ Zapdos — lightning-bird, inside the Power Plant (Route 10 — Surf from Route 9 near the entrance to Rock Tunnel).
  • 🔥 Moltres — fire-bird, deep inside Victory Road on the way to the League.
  • 🟣 Mewtwo — the strongest Pokémon in the game! Found in the Cerulean Cave (next to Cerulean City) only after you become Champion. Use the Master Ball!
💡 Tip: all four are very strong — weaken them to a sliver of HP, put them to sleep, then throw Great Balls or Ultra Balls!

👑The Elite Four — the final 5 battles

Five battles, back to back, with no Pokémon Center in between! Heal your team to full and stock up on Potions, Revives, and Full Heals before you go in. Bring a team with different types.

Lorelei 🧊Uses: Ice + Water

Use ⚡ Electric, 🥊 Fighting, or 🪨 Rock. Watch out for her Cloyster — very tough!

Bruno 🥊Uses: Fighting + Rock

Use 💧 Water, 🌿 Grass, or 🔮 Psychic. Avoid Normal moves!

Agatha 👻Uses: Ghost + Poison

Use 🔮 Psychic or 🌋 Ground. Her Gengar is fast — hit hard!

Lance 🐲Uses: Dragon + Flying

Use 🧊 Ice (super effective!) or ⚡ Electric. His Dragonite is the toughest yet.

Rival (Champion) 👑Uses: All sorts

He has 6 Pokémon including a fully-evolved starter. Bring a balanced team and lots of healing items!

🏆 Beat all 5 → you're the Pokémon Champion! Your trainer goes on the Hall of Fame. Now you can catch Mewtwo in the Cerulean Cave!

💡Useful tips when you get stuck

Can't beat a trainer? Walk around in tall grass for a bit — battling wild Pokémon makes yours level up. Stronger Pokémon = easier wins.
Can't find where to go? Check the Town Map (Start menu → Map). It shows every place — press A on a city to see its name.
Lost in a cave? Most caves have one path. Walk along every wall until you find the next door or ladder.
Can't pass the sleeping Snorlax? You need the Poké Flute — finish the Pokémon Tower in Lavender Town first.
Guard won't let you into Saffron? He's thirsty! Buy a Fresh Water, Soda Pop, or Lemonade from the vending machines at the top of the Celadon Department Store, then talk to him.
Cave too dark to see? You need HM05 Flash — get it from Oak's Aide on Route 2 after you've caught about 10 Pokémon.
Tree in the way? You need HM01 Cut — get it from the SS Anne Captain in Vermilion City.
Boulder in the way? You need HM04 Strength — get it from the Fuchsia Warden (bring the Gold Teeth from the Safari Zone).
Water in the way? You need HM03 Surf — find it in the Safari Zone's secret house.
Out of money? Sell Nuggets 💎 (you get one from Nugget Bridge) or fight a few trainers — they pay when you win.
Out of Poké Balls? Buy them at any Pokémart (the blue-roofed shop in every city).
Stuck on a quiz question? The game gives you a hint after the first wrong answer — read it carefully and try again!

📈The clever part (a note for Mom & Dad)

The further Ethan travels, the more badges he earns — and the game quietly raises the school grade level of the questions to match:
  • 0–2 badges → Grade 1 questions
  • 2–4 badges → Grade 2
  • 4–6 badges → Grade 3
  • 6–7 badges → Grade 4
  • 8 badges → Grade 5
So just by playing the adventure, Ethan is moving up grades — it never feels like a test.
Want to skip ahead? Use ☰ Menu → Focus to lock the grade or subject. Pick the level you'd like him to practise today!
Stuck on a question or two? Talk to Nurse Joy in any Pokémon Center, or Professor Oak — they give free quiz practice with no battle, and they explain the answer when you miss.
See progress: ☰ Menu → Report Card shows correct/total by subject and grade, and shows badges he's earned.

🌟Helpful features (tap ☰ Menu)

🗣️ Read aloud — the game speaks each question and the choices out loud, in a friendly voice. Perfect before Ethan can read fast. There's a Voice button to switch the speaker.
🎯 Focus — choose a Level (grade) and a Subject to practise. Leave it on Auto / Any to let the game pick.
📊 Report Card — see how he's doing by subject and grade, which questions he finds tricky, and earn ⭐ badges for mastering a subject.

👨‍🏫For the teacher (a note for Mom)

You don't need to know Pokémon at all. Here's the easy way to use it:

1. In ☰ Menu → Focus, set the Level to Ethan's grade and pick a Subject you want to work on today.
2. Turn on Read aloud so he can hear every question.
3. Play together — when he misses one, read the hint with him and let him try again. Praise the streak!
4. Once a week, open the Report Card to see what to practise next. This question bank below shows every question, so you can pre-teach.
