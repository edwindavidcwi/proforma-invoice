🌟What is this game?

It looks like a Game Boy adventure, but it's really a learning game. As your child plays and battles, the game asks a school question — answer it right and the move works! Get it wrong and it gives a friendly hint and lets you try again. Nobody is ever punished for a mistake.

💚 The goal: Ethan practises reading, maths, and more without it feeling like homework.

▶️Getting started

  1. Open Pokemon Quiz (the Windows app, or the app on the phone).
  2. Press Start (the Enter key, or tap Start) to begin a new game and follow the short intro.
  3. Walk around with the arrow keys. When a battle begins, choose FIGHT then a move — that's when a question pops up!

🗺️Your Pokémon adventure (how to play)

You are a young trainer starting out in your home town. Your big quest: travel across the land, earn 8 gym badges, and become the Champion! Along the way you catch and train Pokémon — and every move you make is powered by answering a school question.

💚 You can't lose for good. Faint a battle? You wake up safe at the last town with full health and no money lost. So it's always safe to explore.

👟Where to go — step by step

  1. Pallet Town (home). Visit Professor Oak and pick your first Pokémon — 🌿 Bulbasaur (easiest start), 🔥 Charmander, or 💧 Squirtle.
  2. Walk up Route 1 to Viridian City to buy Poké Balls (to catch Pokémon) and Potions.
  3. Go through Viridian Forest to Pewter City — beat Brock for Badge 1.
  4. Cross Mt. Moon to Cerulean City — beat Misty for Badge 2.
  5. Find the move Cut ✂️ on the big ship (S.S. Anne), then beat Lt. Surge in Vermilion City for Badge 3.
  6. Pass through Rock Tunnel and Lavender Town to Celadon City — beat Erika for Badge 4.
  7. Get the Poké Flute in Lavender Town to wake the giant sleeping Snorlax blocking the road.
  8. Reach Fuchsia City — beat Koga for Badge 5. (Explore the Safari Zone here!)
  9. In Saffron City, beat Sabrina for Badge 6.
  10. Use Surf 🌊 to reach Cinnabar Island — beat Blaine for Badge 7.
  11. Return to Viridian City — its gym is now open. Beat Giovanni for Badge 8.
  12. Climb Victory Road, beat the Elite Four and your Rival — you're the Champion! 🏆

⚔️Type tips (what beats what)

Pick a move that is strong against the other Pokémon and it does extra damage. The easy three:

💧 Water beats Fire 🔥 🔥 Fire beats Grass 🌿 🌿 Grass beats Water 💧 ⚡ Electric beats Water 💧 🌋 Ground beats Electric ⚡
💡 Tip: keep two or three different types of Pokémon on your team so you always have something strong to send out.

📈The clever part (a note for Mom & Dad)

The further Ethan travels, the more badges he earns — and the game quietly raises the school grade level of the questions to match. So just by adventuring he is moving up grades, and it never feels like a test.
Special moves to find unlock new paths: Cut ✂️ (clears small trees), Surf 🌊 (cross water), Strength 💪 (push boulders), Flash 🔦 (light up dark caves).
Stuck? Talk to Nurse Joy in any Pokémon Center and Professor Oak — they give free quiz practice with no battle, and explain the answer when you miss.

🌟Helpful features (tap ☰ Menu)

🗣️ Read aloud — the game speaks each question and the choices out loud, in a friendly voice. Perfect before Ethan can read fast. There's a Voice button to switch the speaker.
🎯 Focus — choose a Level (grade) and a Subject to practise. Leave it on Auto / Any to let the game pick.
📊 Report Card — see how he's doing by subject and grade, which questions he finds tricky, and earn ⭐ badges for mastering a subject.

👨‍🏫For the teacher (a note for Mom)

You don't need to know Pokémon at all. Here's the easy way to use it:

1. In ☰ Menu → Focus, set the Level to Ethan's grade and pick a Subject you want to work on today.
2. Turn on Read aloud so he can hear every question.
3. Play together — when he misses one, read the hint with him and let him try again. Praise the streak!
4. Once a week, open the Report Card to see what to practise next. This question bank below shows every question, so you can pre-teach.
